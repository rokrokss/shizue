The quiet comeback of the night train

By Mara Lindqvist · 9 min read

For decades, sleeper trains seemed destined for the history books. Budget airlines were faster, cheaper and everywhere. Yet across Europe, overnight routes are quietly filling up again — and operators are scrambling to add carriages.

The appeal is simple: board after dinner in one capital, wake up in another, and skip the airport entirely.
