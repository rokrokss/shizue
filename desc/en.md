🌸 Shizue puts an AI assistant in Chrome's side panel. Watch videos and read web pages with the original and the translation together, let Shizue click through a task in your tab, chat without switching tabs, and summarize what you're reading in one click.

Shizue is free and open source (MIT). Sign in with ChatGPT to use your plan, or use your own API key from the AI provider you choose and pay that provider only for what you use. You can also use a model that runs on your own computer, with no key and no cost. Shizue itself has no account, no subscription, and no server.


✨ Features

🔐 Sign in with ChatGPT
- Just sign in and use the plan you already have, no API key needed

🎬 Bilingual video captions
- YouTube: captions are translated as you watch and shown under the original line. Press A or D to jump to the previous or next one
- Twitch: live streams and VODs, from the streamer's captions or speech recognition. Chrome's built-in translation runs on your device at no cost
- Netflix: when a title has subtitles in your language, they appear under the original. Otherwise they're translated ahead of playback
- Also Disney+, Prime Video, HBO Max, X, Udemy, Coursera, TED and Vimeo, and live captions in Google Meet, Microsoft Teams and Zoom meetings

🕹️ Browser control
- Tell Shizue what to do and it clicks, types, chooses and scrolls in your tab, one step at a time, with each step shown in the panel
- It asks you before anything that sends, deletes or pays, and never types into password or card fields
- Booking, contact and checkout forms that a page embeds from another site work too
- Start it from the top of Shizue's menu on the page

💬 Side panel chat
- Open it anywhere with Ctrl+Shift+E (Cmd+Shift+E on Mac)
- Attach images and go back to earlier chats from the history

📋 Page summaries
- Get a summary in one click, then ask follow-up questions about it

🌐 Bilingual web pages
- The translation appears under each paragraph, so the original stays in place

🎯 Right-click menu
- Selected text: translate, explain, summarize, or fix the grammar
- Images: describe them, extract their text, or translate them. Translate every image on a page at once for manga and webtoons
- Whole page: translate or summarize

📝 Memos
- Notes that save as you type, with pinning and search

🎨 Also
- A token usage chart that shows how much each model has used
- Light and dark themes
- Interface in 23 languages


🔒 Privacy
- Sign-in details, keys, chat history, and memos stay in your browser
- When you use a feature, only the content it needs goes straight from your browser to the AI provider you chose
- Browser control reads the tab it works in as text: never password or card fields, and no screenshots
- With a local model, nothing leaves your computer
- No analytics or tracking
- Source code: https://github.com/rokrokss/shizue


🚀 Getting started
1. Install Shizue and press Ctrl+Shift+E (Cmd+Shift+E on Mac) to open the side panel
2. Sign in with ChatGPT, paste your API key, or connect a model running on your computer
3. Open a site and start asking
